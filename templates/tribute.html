The Story of 1 Crore Saplings

The spark for this monumental initiative was ignited by former President Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam. During a meeting, Dr. Kalam shared a powerful message with the renowned actor and environmentalist, Vivek. He said, "If you want to achieve something big, you must have single-minded devotion to your goal and never be disheartened by failures." Dr. Kalam urged him to use his massive fan base not just for entertainment, but for a cause that would outlive them all—planting trees.

Moved by these words, Vivek Sir launched the Green Kalam Project. He didn't just advocate for a greener earth; he got his hands dirty. He traveled across schools, colleges, and villages, inspiring the youth to plant saplings. His mission was clear: to plant 1 crore (10 million) saplings. Though his journey was cut short in 2021, his legacy continues to thrive in the millions of trees bearing protective shade across the nation. Through our platform, we invite you to take the baton and continue this noble mission.
